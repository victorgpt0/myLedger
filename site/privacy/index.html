Privacy Policy

myLedger (Android, com.waystar.myLedger) · Effective 26 November 2026

In short: myLedger reads only your M-PESA messages, keeps everything on your phone, and sends your data nowhere.

What myLedger does

myLedger builds a personal record of your M-PESA transactions (balance, payments, income and fees) so you can see how you spend. It does this by reading the confirmation SMS that M-PESA sends to your phone.

What we access

  • SMS messages from M-PESA only. With your permission (Android's READ_SMS and RECEIVE_SMS), the app reads messages from the M-PESA sender ID, both existing messages and new ones as they arrive. Messages from any other sender, including your personal conversations, are skipped and are never read into the app.
  • From each M-PESA message, the app stores the transaction details (reference code, amount, counterparty, balance, fee, date and time) and the message text, so it can show and recalculate your history.

Where your data goes

  • Nowhere. All data stays on your device. myLedger has no servers and no accounts. It does not upload, share, sell or transfer your SMS or transaction data to anyone.
  • Your data is not included in Android cloud backup or device-to-device transfer. On a new phone, the app rebuilds your history from the M-PESA messages on that phone.
  • Your data is not used for advertising, profiling, credit scoring, or any purpose other than showing you your own spending.

App updates

myLedger uses Google Play's in-app update service to check whether a newer version is available. That check is handled by Google Play under Google's Privacy Policy and involves no SMS or transaction data.

Your choices

  • Turn off SMS access at any time in Settings › Apps › myLedger › Permissions. The app then stops reading messages.
  • Hide your balance on the home screen with the eye button. The setting is remembered on your device.
  • Uninstalling the app permanently deletes everything it stored.

Children

myLedger is not directed at children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the effective date above and, if the change is significant, tell you in the app.
